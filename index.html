Three things worth checking yourself
01

Real signing, not a mockup

Every agent identity in this system is backed by a real cryptographic wallet and a real signature. Nothing here is simulated for show.

02

Policy that can't be widened by accident

An account sets the outer limit. A team can only tighten it. An agent can only tighten it further. No config change anywhere in the chain can quietly grant more spending power than the account allows.

03

Proven across rails, not just claimed

One agent, one $10/day limit. $7 spent on one rail blocks a further $5 spend attempt on a completely different rail, because the policy engine tracks total spend, not spend per rail. Watch it happen above.
